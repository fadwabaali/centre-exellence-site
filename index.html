IFRAN OUTING

On a crisp morning, we set off for Ifrane, a charming town in the Middle Atlas. Its Swiss-like appearance, with neat streets and European-style chalets, greeted us. We explored Al Akhawayn University, admired the iconic lion statue, and relaxed by Dayet Aoua Lake. We enjoyed the peaceful surroundings, sharing stories and laughter. Later, we warmed up in a cozy café with hot drinks and pastries. The atmosphere and breathtaking nature made Ifrane the perfect getaway, leaving us refreshed and connected.

July, 2021

ABDESLIMAN OUTING

One warm afternoon, we ventured to Abdesliman, a hidden gem nestled in the rolling hills surrounding our city. The journey itself was wonderful, past stunning views, reaching the tranquility of Abdesliman. We hiked, explored the clear streams, and enjoyed a picnic lunch. We were relaxed later, through the quiet lake for reflection. The combination of good company, tranquility and natural beauty left us feeling at peace.

March, 2022

FOOTBALL MATCHES

Every week, we gathered for our football matches, the highlight of our days. The worn-out field felt like home. We spent hours playing, mixing friendly rivalry, competition and laughter. Whether celebrating an amazing goal or laughing at a missed shot, we always encouraged each other. After each game, we sat together, catching our breath, sharing moments that strengthened our bond. These matches weren't just about football—they were memories we'd cherish forever.
